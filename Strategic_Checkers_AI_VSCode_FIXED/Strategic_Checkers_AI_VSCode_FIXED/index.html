What happens when the AI makes a move?

The visible board is the interface. The intelligence is produced by the search engine inside this project.

1Read board state

The current 8 × 8 position and side to move are represented in memory.

2Generate legal turns

The rules engine enforces captures, complete multi-jumps and promotion.

3Search future replies

Minimax explores alternating AI and human responses.

4Prune branches

Alpha–Beta skips branches that cannot change the final choice.

5Evaluate positions

Material, kings, mobility, advancement, center and safety are scored.

6Play best move

The best legal result found is validated and committed exactly once.

Important for the project review: this is classical Artificial Intelligence, not a downloaded set of pre-made moves and not a machine-learning model. The decision is recalculated from the current board every turn.

Rules & How to Play

These are the exact American / English checkers rules enforced by the game engine for both the human player and the AI.

Red = You · You move firstBlack = AI♛Crown = King
1

Board and turns

The game uses an 8 × 8 board with 12 pieces per side. Only dark squares are playable. Red moves first, then turns alternate.

2

Normal movement

A normal man moves one dark square diagonally forward into an empty square. A king may move one diagonal square in either direction.

!

Captures are mandatory

If any capture is available, a normal non-capturing move is not allowed. The interface highlights only legal choices.

↻

Multi-jump = one complete turn

After a jump, if the same piece can capture again, it must continue. Several jump animations can occur, but the complete chain is recorded as one move and one turn.

5

Capturing

A man jumps diagonally forward over one adjacent opponent piece into the empty square immediately beyond it. Captured pieces are removed.

♛

Kings

When crowned, a king can move and capture diagonally forward or backward. This project uses standard one-square/jump kings, not flying kings.

7

Promotion

When a man reaches the far king row, it is promoted to a king. Under this implemented rule set, reaching the king row ends that capture turn.

8

Choosing captures

If more than one legal capture chain is available, the player may choose any of the complete legal chains offered by the rules engine.

9

How to win

You win when the opponent has no pieces remaining or has no legal move. The AI wins under the same condition.

=

Draw safeguards

The game declares a draw after three occurrences of the same position with the same player to move, or after 80 quiet turns without a capture or man movement.

↶

Undo

Undo restores the position before your previous human + AI round. During an unfinished capture chain, Undo cancels the uncommitted preview.

●

How to move in the UI

Select a highlighted red piece, then select a highlighted destination. Green marks a legal landing square; red marks a capture destination.

How the AI Works

This page is included so the AI implementation is clear during the project review. The game board is only the visualization; the move decision is calculated by the project code.

1State representation

Every piece and empty square is encoded as a board state.

2Legal-move generator

Only rule-correct complete turns enter the search tree.

3Minimax

The AI maximizes its position while assuming the human will choose strong replies.

4Alpha–Beta Pruning

Unnecessary branches are cut without changing the Minimax result for the searched depth.

5Heuristic

Non-terminal positions are scored using pieces, kings, mobility, advancement, center control and safety.

6Search optimizations

Iterative deepening, move ordering and a transposition cache improve practical search.

Why this qualifies as AI: the black move is not hard-coded. A different board state produces a different search tree. The engine reasons over possible future actions and opponent responses, then chooses a legal move based on the evaluated outcome.
